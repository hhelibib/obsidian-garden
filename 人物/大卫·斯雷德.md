## 人物介绍

### 生平
David Aldrin Slade (born 26 September 1968) is a British film director who began his career making music videos. His work includes videos for such musicians as Aphex Twin, Rob Dougan, System of a Down, Stone Temple Pilots, Tori Amos, and Muse.
His first feature film, Hard Candy, was released in 2005 by Lions Gate Entertainment, who purchased the independent movie at the Sundance Film Festival. He went on to direct the vampire movie 30 Days of Night in 2007. Slade directed The Twilight Saga: Eclipse, the third film in the Twilight film series. Slade will be directing a reboot to the 2003 Superhero film Daredevil.
