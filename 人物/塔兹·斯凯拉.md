## 人物介绍

### 生平
Born in the Canary Islands to an Arab father and British mother; Taz made his feature film debut as Dawes in ''The Kill Team'' directed two-time Oscar nominated Dan Krauss. His Stage Debut as Miles in #Warheads at The Park Theatre, in London. Other notable work includes playing the lead role of ''Leonard Vole'' in Agatha Christie's ''Witness For The Prosecution'' on The West-End and Jin in Dean Devlin's ''The Deal''.
