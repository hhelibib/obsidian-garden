## 人物介绍

### 生平
Nadine Bhabha was born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Her mother is London-born Guyanese, and her father is South African. She is an actor known for her roles in Letterkenny (2016), This Hour Has 22 Minutes (1992), and About Sex (2020). Nadine graduated with honors with a BFA in Acting from York University. She won Best Supporting Actress for her role as Sandrine in the award-winning web series, Barbelle. As a comedian and writer, she has produced her own work, and is a featured writer on This Hour Has 22 Minutes.
