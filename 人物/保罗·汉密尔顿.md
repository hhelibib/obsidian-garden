## 人物介绍

### 生平
Born in Scotland, Paul has been working as an international actor for more than 35 years.
He performed in The Histories by Michael Boyd, which won multiple Olivier awards. Other theatre credits include Anthony and Cleopatra (Michael Boyd, RSC), Holly Warriors, (James Dacre Globe Theatre), Peribanez (Rufus Norris, Young Vic Theatre), A Street Car Named Desire (Royal Lyceum Theatre), King Lear (David Farr, RSC), and more.
Film and TV credits include Bridget Jones's Diary, The Legend of Tarzan, Last Night In Soho, VERA (ITV), New Worlds (Channel 4), HENRY IV - PARTS I & II (BBC Television) and The Long Film (BBC Television).
