## 人物介绍

### 生平
Susan "Sue" Rose is an American artist, cartoonist, television producer, voice actress and writer. She is known for co-creating the character Fido Dido with Joanna Ferrone and creating the children's television programs Pepper Ann, Angela Anaconda (with Joanna Ferrone), and Unfabulous.
