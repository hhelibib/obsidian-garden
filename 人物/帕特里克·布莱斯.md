## 人物介绍

### 生平
Patrick Brice holds a BFA in Film from the California Institute of the Arts. His thesis film Maurice premiered at Rotterdam International Film Festival and was awarded the Grand Jury Prize for Best Short Documentary at the Florida Film Festival. Creep, his first feature-film as director/writer/actor (co-starring Mark Duplass) was produced with Blumhouse Productions (Paranormal Activity, Insidious). After its world premiere at SXSW, Creep was picked up for distribution by RADIUS-TWC. He lives and works in Los Angeles.
