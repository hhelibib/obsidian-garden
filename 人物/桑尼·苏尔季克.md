## 人物介绍

### 生平
Sunny Suljic is an American child actor and skateboarder.[1][2] He played Gabriel in the 2015 film 1915, and Bob in the 2017 film The Killing of a Sacred Deer.[3][4] He also did voice work and motion capture for Atreus, the son of Kratos in the 2018 video game, God of War. He is also the lead actor in the upcoming movie Mid-90s directed by Jonah Hill.[5]
