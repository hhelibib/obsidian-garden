## 人物介绍

### 生平
Jonathan Tropper was born on 19 February 1970 in Riverdale, Bronx, New York City, New York, USA. He is a writer and producer, known for Your Friends & Neighbors (2025), Banshee (2013) and The Wrecking Crew (2026). He has been married to Stephanie Abram since 13 November 2016. They have one child. He was previously married to Elizabeth A. Parker.
