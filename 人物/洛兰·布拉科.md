## 人物介绍

### 生平
Lorraine Bracco is an American actress. She is best known for her performances as Karen Friedman Hill in the 1990 Martin Scorsese film Goodfellas, for which she was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress, and as Dr. Jennifer Melfi on the HBO series The Sopranos.
