## 人物介绍

### 生平
About
Vlogger and impressionist who gained fame after his "Matt Smith Impression" video went viral in May 2014. As a result, he attracted the attention of RadioTimes, BuzzFeedUK, and a number of UK news outlets. He has since gained more than 110,000 subscribers on his self-titled YouTube channel.
Before Fame
Though he enjoyed making videos starting at the age of seven, he had never considered posting them to the internet until 2014.
Trivia
He originally sought to become a professional soccer player but after an injury could no longer play. Regardless, he still went on to become a Gold Sports Ambassador for Adidas' Youth Sports Trust at the 2012 London Olympics.
Family Life
He was born Jacob Stanley Dudman in Surrey, England but later grew up in Ripon with his younger sisters Sophie and Rosie.
Associated With
To accompany his impression of Doctor Who star Matt Smith, he posted an impression of successor Peter Capaldi in June 2015.
