## 人物介绍

### 生平
Bo Poraj is an actor and writer. He trained at RADA and has worked extensively in theatre in the West End, Broadway, Royal National Theatre and RSC but is perhaps best known for his television work in Miranda, Musketeers and Newark, Newark. Film credits include The Boat That Rocked, Enigma and The Queen Of Sheeba's Pearls. He has written for Eastenders (BBC), Doctors (BBC) and Ransom (CBS.)
