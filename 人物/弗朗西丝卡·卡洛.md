## 人物介绍

### 生平
Francesca spent her childhood on films and television sets with her parents, and began acting at the age of seven. She enthusiastically followed her passion, studying as a child at the Academy of Dramatic Arts, and later with the Moscow Art Theater School. She has worked with theaters such as New York Stage and Film, as well as with acclaimed international director Adolf Shapiro. She enjoys baking, and is an avid martial artist, with a 2nd Degree Black Belt in Tae Kwon Do.
