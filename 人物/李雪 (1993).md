## 人物介绍

### 生平
Lee Seol (Korean: 이설; April 13, 1993), born Kang Min-jung (강민정), is a South Korean actress. She is best known for her role in the television series Less Than Evil (2018–2019), which earned her a Baeksang Arts Award nomination.
