## 人物介绍

### 生平
Lily LaTorre is a delightful young actor who has already worked with the likes of directors Jeffery Walker in "The Clearing" alongside Kate Mulvaney and Miranda Otto, as well as Daina Reid in "Run Rabbit Run", playing a leading role, Mia, opposite Sarah Snook. She was the face of a major Telstra campaign for a number of years and has appeared in countless high-profile commercials in Australia and the US. Brands have included Fox Sports, Myer, Big W, Woolies, Banana Boat, Queensland Health and Breville, to name just a few. Lily's short films include "You and Me, Before and After" (dir. Madeleine Gottlieb) and "Jake's 7th Birthday" (dir. Ariel Martin). Having started in the industry at a very young age, Lily has an excellent Standard American and English accent. Lily loves working alongside her brother Jack and spending time with her family and dog in coastal country Australia, dancing, playing piano and singing.
