## 人物介绍

### 生平
Oleg Almazov was born on October 16, 1972 in Leningrad (Soviet Union). He studied at the Institute of Telecommunications named after Bonch-Bruevich. In 1996 he graduated from LGITMiK (now the St. Petersburg Theatrical Academy). Almazov was DJ of "Eldoradio" (10 years on the air).
