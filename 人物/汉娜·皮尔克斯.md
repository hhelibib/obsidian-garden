## 人物介绍

### 生平
Pilkes was born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. She grew up in New York City and used to entertain her sister by dressing up in costumes and act like a street performer. She moved to Los Angeles at the age of 20. She is of Dutch descent through her dad.
Pilkes made her on-screen debut as Robin in the drama film The Woodsman starring Kevin Bacon. She trained at The Second City in Los Angeles. She gained popularity for her comedy on the podcast Comedy Bang! Bang!. Her best known role in her career has been as Josie in the sitcom Leanne.
