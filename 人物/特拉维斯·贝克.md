## 人物介绍

### 生平
Travis Beck is an Actor, Producer and Location Manager, who co-starred opposite Mark Rolston and Wolfgang Bodison in an upcoming boxing film called Pug, where he plays the comic-relief character, Hipster Luke. He is from Lincoln, Nebraska, and writes and produces films and TV with his wife, Suzanne LaChasse. Their most recent series, Madame Hula, a proof of concept short, is finishing up Post, and will be headed to Festivals in 2026.
