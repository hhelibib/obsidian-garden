## 人物介绍

### 生平
奥拉维尔·达里·奥拉夫松是出生于於美國的冰島演員，製片人和編劇。他因《兒童》，《 XL》，《被困》，《炸藥》和《深海》而聞名。
Ólafur Darri was born in Connecticut, USA in 1973. He has lived in Iceland for most of his life and works full time as an actor. He does a lot of theatre work as well as films and he is one of the founders of the Vesturport theatre company, which has performed around the world.
