## 人物介绍

### 生平
Rikke Louise Andersson was born on February 13, 1972 in Copenhagen, Denmark. She is known for her work on Nattevagten (1994), Hævnen (2010) and Bleeder (1999). She has been married to Kim Bodnia since 2013. They have three children.
