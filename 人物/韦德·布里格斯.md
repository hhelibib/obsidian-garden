## 人物介绍

### 生平
Wade Briggs graduated from the Western Australian Academy of Performing Arts (WAAPA) in 2010 and makes his television debut in PLEASE LIKE ME.
In Perth, he appeared in the Black Swan Theatre Company’s production of The Damned. His additional theatre credits include K.I.J.E for the Tamarama Rock Surfers and The Real World for the National Play Festival. Wade’s short film credits include Wreckyard, which was officially selected for competition at the Cannes International Film Festival.
