### 生平
C. Robert Cargill was born on 8 September 1975 in San Antonio, Texas, USA. He is a producer and writer, known for The Black Phone (2021), Doctor Strange (2016) and The Gorge (2025). He has been married to Jessica Cargill since 4 May 2002.
