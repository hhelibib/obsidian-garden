### 生平
Casper knopf is a young actor based in the UK. He is best know for his work on 'Halo' where he plays the role of Young John 117, appearing in episodes 1 - 6. He is also known for playing Harry in ITV drama Our House. Soon to be released on Netflix Casper plays the role of Tommy McCabe in Rough Diamonds, a crime drama set in the diamond district of Antwerp. Casper also worked on How To Date Billy Walsh due to be released this yr on Amazon. As well as acting Casper loves skateboarding, gymnastics and Karate.
