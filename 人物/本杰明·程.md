## 人物介绍

### 生平
Benjamin McLean Cheng is a British-Chinese actor, voiceover artist, model and Internet personality born in Reading, Berkshire of mixed heritage. He graduated from the University of Bath with a Bachelor of Science (BSc) and trained at Middleweek Newton Drama Academy from 2022 to 2023.
