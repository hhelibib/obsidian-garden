## 人物介绍

### 生平
Katherine Kelly was born on November 19, 1979 in Barnsley, Yorkshire, England as Katherine Bridget Kelly. She is an actress, known for Coronation Street (1960), Best Possible Taste: The Kenny Everett Story (2012) and Coronation Street: Romanian Holiday (2009). She has been married to Ryan Clark since August 20, 2013. They have one child.近期主要角色是在英剧《塞尔福里奇先生》中饰演贵妇“梅夫人”。
