## 人物介绍

### 生平
Georgui Kasaev is an actor and stuntman and a former trick rider for the Moscow Circus. He is a professional mixed martial artist with black belts in Tae Kwon Do, Brazilian Jiujitsu, and a brown belt in Judo. After ending his career as a professional fighter he worked diligently to adapt his hand-to-hand combat experience to stage combat. He has performed his own stunts in Macgyver, Stranger Things, and True Lies. He has also choreographed stunts for music videos and would be excited to perform stunts for your production as well. Comfortable on horseback, he is perfect for any action role. Whether it be Westerners, Science fiction, or martial arts projects. He was educated in the actors' craft by George Howard Adams and Zila Glory from TPS. he continues to work on his craft through acting classes and improvisation classes. Recently he has completed 4 levels of improvisation under Kelly Petronis and regularly performs with the improvisational group Gorgeous George based in Athens Ga on a weekly basis. He speaks three languages: Russian, Spanish, and English. He would be perfect for any villainous role you may have that requires an eastern European accent.
