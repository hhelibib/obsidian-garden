## 人物介绍

### 生平
Hunter McVey (born April 14, 1999) is an American actor and model who portrays Blue in ABC's 9-1-1: Nashville. McVey is an Instagram star, model, and fitness influencer who is known for his physique and workout vlogs. He has accumulated over 500,000 followers to his hunter_mcvey Instagram account. He is also known for selling exclusive content on his website. 9-1-1: Nashville is McVey's professional acting debut.
