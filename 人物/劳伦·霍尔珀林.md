## 人物介绍

### 生平
Lauren Halperin is an actor most recently known for her work on Donald Glover's Atlanta, her breakout role as Bella Danvers on Netflix's Insatiable, and starring opposite Anna Kendrick in Disney's Noelle. In 2022, you can catch Lauren in multiple productions sharing the screen with Samuel L. Jackson, Rebel Wilson, Michiel Huisman and Charlie Day, and working with directors Ava DuVernay, Mark Boal, and Alex Hardcastle - highlighting her abilities in both comedy and drama. Lauren loves riding her bike, having game night with friends, and listening to Ray LaMontagne songs.
