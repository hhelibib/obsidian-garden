When Finn appeared as Riot Boy in the drama thriller film Offender by Ron Scalpello, he still stayed in the background of his brother, Joe Cole.
In 2014, Finn got the role of Michael Gray in the drama TV show about a Shelby criminal family Peaky Blinders. In that show, he also played with his brother (he portrayed John Shelby) and such famous actors as Cillian Murphy and Paul Anderson. His character didn't remember his family because he was removed from their custody at the age of 5, and when he grew up, he became an accountant of the Shelby family.

The next step in Finn's career was his role of Joshua Cody in the American TV show Animal Kingdom. Ellen Barkin, Ben Robson, Jake Weary, and Shawn Hatosy joined him in this crime drama.
