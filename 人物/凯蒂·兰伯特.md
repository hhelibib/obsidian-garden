## 人物介绍

### 生平
凯蒂·兰伯特创作关于女性情感强烈、做出愚蠢决定的电影，因为艺术常常模仿生活。她相信以角色为驱动的喜剧、酷儿意图，以及在每个城市举办密室逃脱。作为制片人，凯蒂的丰富作品集包括为Pink、The Weeknd、Harry Styles和Prince等人的音乐视频，以及超级碗广告;还有那次与Usher在布拉格的合作。作为编剧/导演，凯蒂有两部短片目前正在各大电影节巡回放映;《Eat It》——一部关于我们心碎时所吃东西的电影;以及与乐队Suede合作的《Autofiction》。合作者还包括Wet Leg、Self Esteem和Georgia。
Katie Lambert creates films about women having big feelings and making stupid decisions, because art often imitates life. She believes in character-driven comedy, queer intentions, and doing an escape room in every city she visits. Having started out as a producer, Katie’s extensive portfolio includes music videos for the likes of Pink, The Weeknd, Harry Styles and Prince, to name a few, alongside Super Bowl commercials; and that one time in Prague with Usher. As a writer/director, Katie has two short films which are currently making the festival circuits; ‘Eat It’ – a film about the things we eat when we’re broken-hearted; and ‘Autofiction’, made with the band Suede. Collaborators also include Wet Leg, Self Esteem and Georgia.
