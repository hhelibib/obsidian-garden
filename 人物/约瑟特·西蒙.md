### 生平
Josette Simon was born in 1960 in Leicester, Leicestershire, England as Josette Patricia Simon. She is an actress, known for Wonder Woman (2017), Pokémon: Detective Pikachu (2019) and Red Lights (2012). She was previously married to Mark Padmore.
