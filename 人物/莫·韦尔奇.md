## 人物介绍

### 生平
Mo Welch is a stand up comedian and writer. She trained in improvisation at Second City, Improv Olympic Chicago, The Annoyance Theater and Upright Citizens Brigade. She performs stand up around the world, but resides in Los Angeles.
Her viral comic Blair was turned into a ten part short series on TBS and will be released as a book called "How To Die Alone" on April 16, 2019.
