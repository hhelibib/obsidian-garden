## 人物介绍

### 生平
Daniel Lasker is a Zimbabwean actor and filmmaker. His credits include One Piece Season 2 on Netflix, Raised By Wolves on HBO Max, Noughts & Crosses on BBC One, and London Calling. During his time growing up in Zimbabwe there wasn't an active film industry, so he taught himself how to make his own films, which over time became well received by the Zimbabwean community and various foreign film festivals.
