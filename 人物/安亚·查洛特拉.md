### 生平
Anya Chalotra is an English actress, best known for playing ‘Jennifer Ashman’ in the British drama series ‘Wanderlust.’ Chalotra made headlines in 2018 when she was cast as ‘Yennefer’ in the upcoming ‘Netflix’ fantasy drama ‘The Witcher.’ It is based on a book series that was later turned into a popular video-game series. However, many fans were not happy with the makers’ decision of casting Chalotra as ‘Yennefer.’
