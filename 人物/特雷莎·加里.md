## 人物介绍

### 生平
Los Angeles native, Treisa Gary, is a rare blend of Hollywood talent and transformational coach, making waves both on screen and behind the scenes. Her unexpected journey into acting began during her time as a lead accountant at Telepictures Productions, where executives recognized her natural gift for performance.
Since then, Treisa has brought her authenticity to sets alongside industry icons like Christian Bale, Nicolas Cage, Zac Efron, Rose Byrne, Shemar Moore, Danny DeVito, and Angela Bassett.
Off-camera, she's a sought-after acting coach, helping actors land dream roles with her unique "8 Steps to Working Actor" audition technique-a method that blends technical mastery with the power of mindset and presence.
Treisa's work extends beyond Hollywood. Known for her transformative sound bowl experiences and as an ordained minister, she's helped people clear energetic blocks, navigate life changes, and celebrate their most meaningful milestones.
Whether she's coaching an actor to crush their next audition or guiding someone toward a breakthrough, Treisa's signature blend of humor, wisdom, and grounded energy leaves a lasting impact. You may show up for career guidance, but chances are, you'll leave with a renewed perspective on life.
