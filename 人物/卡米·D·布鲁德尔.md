### 生平
Kamy Bruder is an actor and stuntman based in Los Angeles CA. He is a black belt in Kyokushin Karate, an expert level motorcycle rider (former professional motocross racer), a certified weapons manipulation expert through ITTS, and a fight scene specialist, all which have served him well in various productions across the globe.
