### 生平
Patrick Schwarzenegger was born September 18, 1993 at St. Johns Hospital in Los Angeles, California. Patrick lives in LA and attended high school there. He is co-founder of \"Project360, a hip modern apparel company that donates a portion of its proceeds to worthy charities. Patrick's first movie was The Benchwarmers (2006), and he has since appeared in Stuck in Love (2012), Grown Ups 2 (2013), and Scouts Guide to the Zombie Apocalypse (2015).
