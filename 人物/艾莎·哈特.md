## 人物介绍

### 生平
An exotic and alluring actress, Aiysha Hart is mixed race - British and Middle Eastern. Her first leading role was alongside Paddy Considine in independent feature film, Honour. She was then cast as Princess Ariadne (later Queen) in the big-budget UK television drama series, Atlantis (2013). Hart gained a first class degree in English Literature from Kings College London, before then embarking on post-graduate acting training. She speaks fluent Arabic as well as English.
