## 人物介绍

### 生平
Dimitrius Schuster-Koloamatangi was born in Auckland, New Zealand. He is an actor of Samoan & Tongan Heritage, known for his role as Will Ìlolahia in The Panthers (2021) and his role as Veni in Red, White and Brass (2023)
Dimitrius attended St Peters College in Auckland, graduating in 2018 and was awarded the Kiwa Scholarship to the Auckland University of Technology where he completed a Bachelors of Communication Studies in 2023.
Dimitrius was cast in his first role in 2019 where he played the role of Drew in Jonah (2019). In only his second role, Dimitrius was cast as the Lead in The Panthers (2021) where he was recognized by the Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF) and was selected to be apart of their Prestigious Rising Stars Programme in 2021. Dimitrius also received a nomination for Best Actor at the 2022 NZTV Awards for his performance.
In 2022, Dimitrius was cast in his first Feature Film, Red, White and Brass (2023) playing the co-lead Veni.
