## 人物介绍

### 生平
A member of CAEA and UBCP/ACTRA. With over 20 years of film, theatre and singing experience, Margaret acted in many different roles in theatrical productions such as Much Ado About Nothing, A Doll's House, FOB, M. Butterfly, The King and I, Man of La Mancha, West Side Story, Smile, The Odd Couple, Damn Yankees, Merrily We Roll Along and many more. Other experience include acting teacher, director, producer, voice over actor, script translator and casting director.
Margaret is the only certified instructor of the "Mosiac Acting System" (developed by Jeremy Whelan) in Hong Kong. She also teaches the only On Camera Acting Workshops in Hong Kong that is opened to the public. Margaret is a part-time lecturer at the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts.
Voice over credits (English & Cantonese) include: Naked Weapon, Magic Kitchen, Harry Potter III, Lethal Weapon I & III, The Lion King, Freaky Friday and many more.
