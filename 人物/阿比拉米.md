### 生平
Divya Gopikumar, professionally known as Abhirami (born July 1983) is an Indian actress and reality show judge. She has acted predominantly in Tamil and Malayalam films and few credits in Telugu and Kannada films.Abhirami went on to established herself as an leading actress in the 2000s across the Malayalam and Tamil language industries.
