### 生平
Madeline Brewer was born May 1, 1992 in Pitman, New Jersey. She is a graduate of American Musical and Dramatic Academy in New York City, graduating in 2012. Previously, she has done some work on Broadway - a musical called Liberty. Her TV debut is "Orange Is the New Black" (2013). She currently resides in New York City (2013).
