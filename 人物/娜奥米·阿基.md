## 人物介绍

### 生平
Naomi Ackie (born November 2, 1992) is a British actress. In 2017, she won a BIFA for most promising newcomer. She is best known for her secondary role in Lady Macbeth (2016), her first role in the film industry, which included being nominated twice for the British Independent Film Awards.
