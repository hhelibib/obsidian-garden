## 人物介绍

### 生平
Adrian Molina is an American screenwriter and storyboard artist. He has been at Pixar since 2007, where he started as a 2D animator on Ratatouille. He later moved on to be a storyboard artist, working on Toy Story 3 and Monsters University.
Alma mater: California Institute of the Arts
Movies: Coco
Books: Toy Story 3
Nominations: Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Main Title and Graphic Design
