## 人物介绍

### 生平
Michael was born and raised in Toronto, Ontario, and attended McGill University in Montreal. Shortly after graduating, Michael was accepted into the Actors Studio MFA program at Pace University in New York City but decided to work and train in his hometown of Toronto. Michael has had and continues to have the great pleasure of training extensively with some of the best acting teachers and studios in the city. You can see Michael in television shows such as Workin' Moms (2017) and The Handmaid's Tale (2017), and movies such as Guidance (2014) and Remember (2015).
