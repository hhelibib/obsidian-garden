## 人物介绍

### 生平
Zazie Beetz is an actress. Born in Berlin, Germany to a German Father and African-American mother, she raised in Manhattan speaking both German and English at home. She performed in community theaters and local stages from a young age. She is known for Atlanta (2016), Applesauce (2015, and Finding Her (2017).
