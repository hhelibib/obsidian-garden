## 人物介绍

### 生平
Adam Croasdell (10 July 1980)[1] is a Zimbabwean-born[2][3] British actor. He has appeared on TV in Supernatural, The Chase, Holby City, Peak Practice, London's Burning, Agatha Christie's Cat Among the Pigeons and Ultimate Force.[4] In 2009, he was cast as Doctor Al Jenkins in the BBC soap opera, EastEnders.[5] He has also appeared in films such as The Prince and Me 3: Royal Honeymoon and Attack Force and on the stage.[4]
