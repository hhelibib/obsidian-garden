## 人物介绍

### 生平
Mya has always been a performer at heart. She was born in Victoria, BC., where she began her career as an award-winning Hip Hop dancer. She competed internationally for several years before making the move to Vancouver after high school. Given her enthusiasm for the performing arts, Mya signed up for acting classes and quickly discovered her deep passion for the craft. Some of her recent credits include "My Life with the Walter Boys", "Yellowjackets" (2021), "Fakes" (2022), "Charmed" (2022), and Nickelodeon's musical "The J Team" (2021). Mya finds joy in discovering her potential and pushing herself outside of her comfort zone.
