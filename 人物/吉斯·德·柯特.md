## 人物介绍

### 生平
Gijs De Corte is a Belgian actor born in Ghent. He honed his craft at the MuDa Atheneum for Performing Arts and the Kunsthumaniora in Antwerp, culminating in a Bachelor's degree in Acting from the Amsterdam University of the Arts (ATKA) in 2021.
Gijs has made a significant impact on both television and film. He is best known for his roles as Hugo in the dystopian VRT series Arcadia (2023-2025), Tanguy Leroy in the courtroom drama Assisen: The Insulin Murder (2024), and Matthias in the satirical series Dansaertvlamingen (2023). His versatility is further showcased in appearances on Bathroom Stories (2020), Otis (2024), and Single Bells (2024).
In 2025, Gijs stars as Boris in the Streamz Original series Oh, Otto!, which received the Student Award and Best Short Form Series at the Canneseries festival. His film work includes the short film All at Sea (2024), where he played the lead role.
