## 人物介绍

### 生平
Charlie Rawes hails from England. He was a design student during 1997 at Colchester Institute. Represented by the Gregg Millard Management in London, England, Rawes began his acting career in 2011. His early credits often included playing brawny, nasty and tough characters. His film credits include Gangsters, Guns & Zombies (2012), Jue di tao wang (2016), King Arthur: Legend of the Sword (2017) and Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom (2018). Charlie Rawes has experience as a presenter and voice actor.
