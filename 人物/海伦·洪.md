## 人物介绍

### 生平
Helen Hong is known for Silicon Valley, Never Have I Ever, The Unicorn,The Thundermans on Nickelodeon, and the Coen Brothers' Inside Llewyn Davis. She has guest starred on The Sex Lives of College Girls, Jane the Virgin, Parks and Recreation, and Pretty Little Liars. Helen is a recurring panelist on NPR's Wait Wait Don't Tell Me, and hosts the trivia podcast Go Fact Yourself on LA-ist.
