## 人物介绍

### 生平
Félix de Givry is a French screenwriter, director, and producer. While studying at Sciences Po Paris, he discovered filmmaking when he took on a minor role in Olivier Assayas’s Something in the Air (2012), and later the main role later in Mia Hansen-Løve’s Eden (2014). It is on this set that he met Ugo Bienvenu, with whom he founded the production company Remembers to produce their respective films. He went on to direct several short films (Journée Blanche in 2017, L’Entretien in 2019…), and he co-wrote and produced Arco, an animated film directed by Ugo Bienvenu (released in 2025, it won a César for Best Animation film and was nominated for an Academy Award). Adieu monde cruel is his first narrative feature film.
