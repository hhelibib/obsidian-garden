## 人物介绍

### 生平
Steve Park was born on 4 May 1962 in Vestal, New York, USA. He is an actor, known for Fargo (1996), Do the Right Thing (1989) and The French Dispatch of the Liberty, Kansas Evening Sun (2021). He has been married to Kelly Coffield Park since 31 December 1999. They have two children.
