## 人物介绍

### 生平
Tonya Cornelisse is an actress and writer. She is known forBobby Moresco's Bent (2018), Nick Cassavetes' Yellow (2012), Crave (2012), Parks and Recreation (2009), and NCIS: Naval Criminal Investigative Service (2003). Tonya started acting professionally in the theatre in her early teens. She has studied privately with Harold Guskin & Alan Arkin, and is a lifetime member of The Actors Gym in Los Angeles. Tonya attended NYU's Tisch school of the Arts and has a double major in Psychology. Tonya loves marshmallows and ketchup.
