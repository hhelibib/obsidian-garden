## 人物介绍

### 生平
Jaume Claret Muxart is a Catalonian filmmaker. His works include the short films HER AND I (2020), which was screened in San Sebastián and Rotterdam, and DIE DONAU (2022), which was shown at FIDMarseille and Valladolid International Film Festival. He teaches design and production at institutions including the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya and is a member of the artist collective Arquitectura Filmada.
