## 人物介绍

### 生平
Adam Michael Gold (born January 25th, 1989) is an American actor, writer, and stuntman hailing from Tucson, Arizona. Having established a foothold in Los Angeles as a film actor and stuntman, Adam's unique vocal talents led him to pursue a full-time career in voiceover. With extensive film and voice resumes that span the full spectrum of entertainment--from commercial and theatrical work, to animation and video games, to documentary and audiobook narration--Adam has established a vast client base that includes many of the biggest names in the industry. He is represented and managed by ACM Talent (previously William Morris Endeavor and the Polygon Group).
