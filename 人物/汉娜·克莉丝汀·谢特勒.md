## 人物介绍

### 生平
Hannah Christine Shetler began acting in the theatre when she was 6 years old, and began her professional career at the age of 9. Some of her most notable roles on stage include Helen Keller in "The Miracle Worker", Ramona Quimby in "Romana Quimby", and Jane Banks in "Mary Poppins". Her debut performance on screen was in the short film "The Void" as Clio Sharp which was apart of a filmmaking contest held by Ron Howard. Since then, she has appeared in several indie films, including the 2025 feature film "Lurker" as Kendra, which premiered at the 2025 Sundance Film Festival, as well as co-starring in the DC show "Lanterns" as Maggie, set to premiere in 2026. She is most well known for her work as the titular character in the feature film, 'Waking Karma", alongside Michael Madsen.She studied at several reputable studios and camps, including the Deborah Lemen Acting Studio, AMTC, Grey Studios, Jason La Padura, CSA, Pilot Camp with Greg James Blount, and most recently at The American Musical and Dramatic Academy where she graduated with a BFA in Acting in 2023.
