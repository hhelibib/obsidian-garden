Ella Balinska

Ella Balinska is an English actress.
She is the daughter of television cook and model Lorraine Pascale and musician Count Kazimierz Balinski-Jundzill. Her father is of Polish and English descent. Her mother was born in London, England, to African-Caribbean parents, and was adopted by Audrey and Roger Woodward.
Ella’s paternal grandfather is Count Krystoph Baliński-Jundziłł (the son of Jan Baliński-Jundziłł and Maria Celestyna Niezabytowska). Jan was the son of Ignacy Baliński Jastrzębiec, who was born in Reginów, Rokiciny/Tomaszów Mazowiecki, Łódzkie, Poland, and of Maria Chomętowska Lis. Ella’s great-grandmother Maria was the daughter of Karol Stanisław Niezabytowski Lubicz, who was born in Oleszewiczach, Hrodna, Belarus, and of Zofia Łęska Ostoja.
Ella’s paternal grandmother is Katrina Emmott (the daughter of John/Jack Emmott and Mary Rycroft). Katrina is English, and was born in Southampton, Hampshire, England. John was the son of Willie Emmott.
