## 人物介绍

### 生平
Luke Roberts trained at L.A.M.D.A. in London, graduating in 2000. Prior to that he earned a degree in American Studies from the University of Wales, Swansea and UMASS, Amherst. Although the only actor in the family, Luke comes from an artistic background. His sister teaches art at a number of institutions while his parents run a photographic and fine arts studio in Suffolk where he grew up. His older brother clearly didn't get the memo, studied physics and became a world class professor of radiology.
