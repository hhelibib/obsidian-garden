## 人物介绍

### 生平
Callum is an English actor, he was born on Christmas Eve, 2006.
He signed with a talent agency at the age of 10 and quickly gained his first role, starring alongside his younger sister Olivia in a scripted anthology series produced by Michael Haussman, Jude Law and Pulse films.
In 2018, Callum gained his breakout role starring in the challenging and beautiful role as Max/ Maxine in the miniseries Butterfly. Later that year he was cast to play the role of Karl Thorne in the TV drama Peaky Blinders series 5.
