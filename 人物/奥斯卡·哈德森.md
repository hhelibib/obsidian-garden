## 人物介绍

### 生平
Oscar Hudson (1990) first trained as a social anthropologist before swapping ethnography for the world of low budget music videos in London. It was here he honed his highly distinctive style, making work defined by innovative storytelling forms and a playful sense of dark humour. Today, he is one of the most celebrated & in demand voices working in short form globally, winning 100+ major awards including a Primetime Emmy, Cannes Lions Grand Prix, UKMVA Best Director & the Short Film Audience Award at Rotterdam.
