### 生平
Chloe Breen is a Los Angeles-based actor, writer, and creative producer with a background in film, digital content, and character-driven storytelling that blends sharp comedy with grounded emotional beats. A graduate of the New York Film Academy (BFA), she has worked across independent film, studio productions, and branded content.
Her feature film work includes Obsession (2026), marking her first role in a world wide release feature film. Known for her versatility and natural comedic timing, Chloe is drawn to character-driven stories across genres, with a particular interest in projects that balance emotional depth and sharp wit.
When she’s not on set or writing, she’s usually building the next project she wants to star in.
