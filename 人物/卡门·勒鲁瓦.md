## 人物介绍

### 生平
Carmen Leroi is a director and screenwriter. Her short films, Les Belles Portes (self-produced), Pour Elsa, Sans regret, and La Réputation (co-directed with Emmanuel Mouret) were selected and awarded at festivals in France (Belfort, Côté Court, Brive, Clermont-Ferrand) and internationally. She is currently preparing her first feature film, L’Expérience impossible, and has just completed a new short film, Free Drum Kit.
