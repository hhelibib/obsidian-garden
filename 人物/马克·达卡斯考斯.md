## 人物介绍

### 生平
Mark Alan Dacascos (born February 26, 1964) is an American actor and martial artist.[1] He has won numerous karate and kung fu championships between the ages of 7 and 18. He is best known for his roles as Mani in Brotherhood of the Wolf, Toby Wong in Drive, Ling in Cradle 2 the Grave, the titular role in Crying Freeman and Zero in John Wick: Chapter 3 – Parabellum.
