Mini Bio (1)
Taylor is a Los Angeles based actor with a BFA in Acting from Ithaca College. Originally from San Jose, California, Taylor discovered her passion for acting at a young age at the CMT San Jose. Taylor is known for her co-star roles on About A Boy (2015) and Crazy Ex-Girlfriend (2016). Taylor starred in 'Survivors' by Alissa Dean in a 2015 Hollywood playwright festival and played the role of Judy in a Minneapolis Fringe Festival production of 'Sincerity Forever'. Her Ithaca College credits include: The Crucible (Abigail Williams), Fires in the Mirror (Letty/Roslyn), Rhinoceros (Daisy), The Dream of the Burning Boy (Andrea).
