## 人物介绍

### 生平
Chad Michael Murray is an American actor, spokesperson, writer and former fashion model. He is best known for playing Lucas Scott in The WB/CW drama series One Tree Hill, a recurring role as Tristan Dugray on The WB/CW series Gilmore Girls, Charlie Todd in the fifth season of The WB series Dawson’s Creek and war veteran and SSR Agent Jack Thompson in the Marvel/ABC series Agent Carter. He had starring roles in the films Freaky Friday, A Cinderella Story, House of Wax and Fruitvale Station. He portrayed Edgar Evernever on The CW show Riverdale
