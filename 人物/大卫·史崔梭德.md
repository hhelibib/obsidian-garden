## 人物介绍

### 生平
Devid Striesow was born on 1 October 1973 in Bergen auf Rügen, East Germany [now Germany]. He is an actor, known for Im Westen nichts Neues (2022), Die Fälscher (2007) and 3 (2010). He has been married to Ines Ganzberger since December 2018. They have two children. He was previously married to Francine.
