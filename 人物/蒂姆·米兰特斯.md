## 人物介绍

### 生平
His early short film success led to directing work on Code 37, Zingaburia and Cordon, establishing him as one of the key series directors in Flanders. His feature film debut De Patrick won Best Director at Karlovy Vary and seven Ensor Flemish Film Awards. He has directed an episode of Tales from the Loop and two seasons of Legion as well as episodes of The Terror, The Tunnel and all six episodes of the third season of Peaky Blinders. As lead director on the BBC series The Responder, starring Martin Freeman, he received nominations for the BAFTA, RTS and Emmy awards.
