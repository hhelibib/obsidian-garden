## 人物介绍

### 生平
Peter Diseth started acting at Capital High School in Olympia, WA, where a small role in "The Man Who Came to Dinner" was a big enough experience to change the trajectory of his life, from jock to drama kid. He studied theatre performance at Chapman University (Orange, CA) before finishing his undergraduate at Southern Oregon University (Ashland, OR), where he earned a BFA in Acting and a BS in Directing. Then from Oregon to NYC, NYC to Boston, Boston to a national tour of stages across America, and finally to Albuquerque, NM, where his Film & Television career officially began.
