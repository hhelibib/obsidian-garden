## 人物介绍

### 生平
Ken Marino was born on December 19, 1968 in Long Island, New York, USA. He is an actor and writer, known for Wanderlust (2012), The Ten (2007) and Childrens Hospital (2008). He has been married to Erica Oyama since October 8, 2005. They have two children.
