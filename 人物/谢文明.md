## 人物介绍

### 生平
台湾独立动画导演,作品常探讨人性阴暗面,并以惊悚诡异的东方风格著称,2008《肉蛾天》获圣地亚哥亚洲影展最佳动画片，2012《礼物》获台北电影节最佳动画片，并提名金马奖最佳创作短片。
Joe Hsieh, Taiwan independent animation film director, his work often explores the dark side of human nature, filled with thrilling suspense and styled with an oriental flair. His films were critically acclaimed - Meat Days won Best Animation in the 2008 San Diego Film Festival and The Present in the 2012 Taipei Film Festival and also nominated in the 2012 Golden Horse Awards.
