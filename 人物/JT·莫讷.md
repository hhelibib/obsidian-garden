## 人物介绍

### 生平
Mollner began filmmaking with a series of award winning shorts, music videos and commercials. In 2016, he wrote and directed his debut feature, "Outlaws and Angels" which premiered at the Sundance Film Festival. His follow up, "Strange Darling", premiered at Fantastic Fest in the fall of 2023 to strong critical reviews and will be released exclusively in cinemas in 2024. JT is a proponent of celluloid, and was the very first recipient of the Kodak Auteur Award in 2017.
