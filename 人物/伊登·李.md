## 人物介绍

### 生平
Eden Lee is a Korean-American actor, born and raised in Queens, New York and northeastern Pennsylvania. She graduated with Honors at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte with a Bachelor's in Nursing. While training and growing in her career as a film/TV actor in the Southeast and working on several national commercial campaigns, she continues to work in the Emergency Department to serve her community.
