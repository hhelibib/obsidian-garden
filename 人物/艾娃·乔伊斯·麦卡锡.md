## 人物介绍

### 生平
Ava Joyce McCarthy was born in East London Whitechapel in 2013.
She began her career modelling in 2017 and has worked with some of the best High street brands over the years. Represented by her agency Kids London.
She made her first TV appearance for a Wilko commercial in the summer of 2022
Ava progressed into the acting world at the end of 2022 and landed her debut role in the first 'John Wick' spin off 'The Ballerina' which is set to be released in June 2025.
Since then Ava has been making progress and has secured a part in Disney's 'Andor' season 2. She is now filming for her first main character appearance for a forthcoming TV production.
Ava has a great passion for acting and performing and she is excited to see what the future offers.
