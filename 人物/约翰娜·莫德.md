## 人物介绍

### 生平
The Austrian screenwriter and director made her feature film debut in 2014 with High Performance which won the Audience Award at the Max Ophüls Prize Film Festival. Her film Once Were Rebels premiered at the Zurich Film Festival where it won the Ecumenical Jury Prize before going on to win the awards for Best Director at the Max Ophüls Prize Film Festival in 2020 and the Thomas Pluch Screenwriting Award. She directed the series School of Champions as well as the TV films Zeit zu beten and Ewig Dein.
