## 人物介绍

### 生平
Callie Schuttera is an American-born actress and producer who grew up in Cincinnati Ohio. Callie is known for her iconic role in Zach Cregger's 'Weapons' and her appearances on shows like 'The Rookie' and 'On Call'. Callie earned international acclaim on the festival circuit for her performance in 'Blue', which took home a slew of awards including multiple wins for Best Picture and Best Actress. Callie can also be seen in the family comedy 'The Last Conception' and voices Vivian Lobdow, on the popular Darkest Night podcast. Callie earned a B.F.A. in Acting from the College-Conservatory of Music (CCM). After moving to Los Angeles, she co-created Poison Pictures Film House with her wife, Gabriela Ledesma. Poison Pictures is a leading indie producer of feature films, music videos, and documentaries.
