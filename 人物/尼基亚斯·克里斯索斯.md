## 人物介绍

### 生平
Born in Heidelberg on 25.9.1978, he has written short stories and worked as a DJ. He studied at Surrey Institute of Art & Design in 2001/02, and has been attending the Baden-Württemberg Film Academy since 2002. He took part in the 2005 Berlinale Talent Campus, and attended a Master Class programme at UCLA in Los Angeles.
