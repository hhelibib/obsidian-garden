## 人物介绍

### 生平
LAIA COSTA is the lead in Sebastian Schipper's film Victoria (Germany 2015, 140 min / German, English) world premiered on February 7th in Official Competition at the Berlin International Film Festival 2015.
Winner of the Silver Bear for Outstanding Artistic Contribution, the German Art House Cinema Prize and the Berlinale Morgenpost Reader's Jury Award.
