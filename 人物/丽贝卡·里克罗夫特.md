## 人物介绍

### 生平
Bex Rycroft is a London based director, specializing in high-end scripted comedy-drama, having filmed in Malaysia, South Africa, Costa Rica, Spain, and the US. She has received an RTS Nomination for her first children's series Hank Zipzer, created by and starring Henry Winkler and won a Broadcast Award for her first lead director series So Awkward. She has since directed for Hulu and Showtime, as well as all major UK broadcasters, her latest project is a dark thriller series for Paramount+
She is known for Screw (2022), We Hunt Together (2021), McDonald and Dodds (2020), Malory Towers (2019) and in her previous career as Script Supervisor for Horrible Histories, Peep Show, Four Lions, Episodes, Twenty Twelve, and The Mighty Boosh.
