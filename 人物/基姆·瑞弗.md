## 人物介绍

### 生平
Kim Raver was born on March 15, 1969 in New York City, New York, USA as Kimberly Jayne Raver. She is an actress and producer, known for 24 (2001), Third Watch (1999), Grey's Anatomy (2005) and Night at the Museum (2006). She has been married to Manu Boyer since July 2000. They have two children.
