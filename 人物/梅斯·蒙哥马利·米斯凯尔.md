## 人物介绍

### 生平
Since 2013, Mace has done extensive work in commercial and print for companies like Gatorade, Kraft, Versace, Nike, Kohl's, Eastbay, Chrysler, Walgreens, and Spalding. In 2016, Mace landed a recurring role on the first season of the NBC series Chicago Med. In 2019, he appeared in his first feature film Canal Street.
