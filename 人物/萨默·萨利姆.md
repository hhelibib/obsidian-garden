## 人物介绍

### 生平
Samer Salem is an actor who is most definitely joining the ranks of 'Ones to Watch' in 2022. After reprising his role as Josep, in a standout performance in the critically acclaimed sixth and final season of The Expanse on Amazon Prime, Samer went on to star opposite Keiynan Lonsdale, Dylan Sprouse, and Sarah Hyland in the much-anticipated romantic comedy, My Fake Boyfriend, due in June 2022.
Samer has also starred alongside Elisabeth Moss in the record breaking Emmy nominated fourth season of The Handmaid's Tale, in ABC's medical drama The Good Doctor, as well as in Amazon Prime's breakout hit The Boys, playing super villain Naqib. Additional credits include Condor, Imposters, People of Earth, and Two Sentence Horror Stories for CW.
