## 人物介绍

### 生平
Emily Pendergast was born on 25 February 1987 in Youngstown, Mahoning County, Ohio, USA. She is an actress and writer, known for Veep (2012), Love (2016) and We're Doing Good (2022). She has been married to Cory Pendergast since 8 September 2012.
