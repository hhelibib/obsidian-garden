## 人物介绍

### 生平
Ksenia Daniela Kharlamova is a Toronto based actress and model that started off acting in 2016, landing a role on Amazon Primes "The Boys". Since then she has worked on CBCs "Strays" as Yazmin and Rokus "Slip" as Jasmyn. In 2022, she was cast as Much in the re imagined legend of Robin Hood, "Robyn Hood" created by Director X for Global TV. She has modeled for Fashion Magazine, Fumi the Label, Undivided Creative and appeared in national campaigns such as Sephora and Roots. She is represented by Breann Thordarson and Jessica Martins of Hero Artists.
