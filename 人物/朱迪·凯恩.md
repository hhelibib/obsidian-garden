## 人物介绍

### 生平
Judy was born in San Mateo, California, and began her professional acting career in Los Angeles in 1978 with her first TV appearance on Three's Company. She has been in over 400 commercials, and 160 TV and film projects. She has won numerous awards for coaching and teaching at her studio, Keep it Real Acting Studios, and awards for her performance in MAD MEN garnering the SAG AWARD for the Show. She can be seen this season in Beef and recurring on General Hospital Judy is also a published author of the Commercial Handbook called "I Booked It!" Being an avid Hiker, she wrote a book about her hikes all over the world called LIFE IS LIKE A HIKE...the best view comes after the Hardest climb. Her son Frankie Manes is also an actor.
