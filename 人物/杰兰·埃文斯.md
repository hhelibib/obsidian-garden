## 人物介绍

### 生平
Jaylan Evans is an American Actor. Jaylan is best known for his roles on Power Book III: Raising Kanan, Spirit Rangers, and My Life With the Walter Boys. Born and raised in Greensboro, North Carolina, Jaylan grew up training at Weaver Academy, University of North Carolina School of the Arts, and Elon University, where he recently received his BFA in Music Theatre. While in school, Jaylan studied classical acting abroad in London, United Kingdom, where he grew to love the art of stage/screen combat and the power of writing. Jaylan is passionate about connecting with and representing individuals, who may not have always seen themselves represented, through many creative outlets.
