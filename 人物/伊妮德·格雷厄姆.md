## 人物介绍

### 生平
Enid Graham is a writer and actress living in New York City and a second-year playwright in the Juilliard School's Lila Acheson Wallace American Playwrights Program. Most recently, her short play for Zoom, Do Not Go, My Love, was performed in the Hudson Stage Company's online reading series.
