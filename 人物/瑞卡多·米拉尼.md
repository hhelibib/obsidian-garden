## 人物介绍

### 生平
Riccardo Milani was born in Rome, Italy, in 1958. He started off as an assistant director before making his directorial debut with the classroom comedy AUGURI PROFESSORE in 1997. His most recent films, THANK YOU GUYS (2022) and A WORLD APART (2025), were screened in German theaters. The comedy LIFE IS LIFE had its world premiere in 2025 at the Rome Film Festival, where it was the opening film.
