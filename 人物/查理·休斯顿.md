## 人物介绍

### 生平
Charlie Huston is a novelist and TV/screenwriter. He writes genre stories about damaged people who make the kinds of decisions that send their lives into a ditch that they then have to crawl out of so they can help someone else. His 12 published novels include CAUGHT STEALING, THE MYSTIC ARTS OF ERASING ALL SIGNS OF DEATH, ALREADY DEAD, SLEEPLESS and THE SHOTGUN RULE. He's developed TV projects for HBO, FX, FOX, Sony TV, HBO Max, Tomorrow Studios and others.
