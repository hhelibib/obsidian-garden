## 人物介绍

### 生平
Elizabeth Adams is a Canadian screen and stage actor best known for her performance as Ello in Wayward (Netflix). She is an alumna of TMU's BFA Acting program and the Stratford Festival's Birmingham Conservatory for classical theatre. She was the recipient of the Mary Savidge Award at the 2023 Guthrie Awards.
