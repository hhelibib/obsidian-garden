## 人物介绍

### 生平
Regé-Jean Page is a British actor. He played the role of Chicken George in the 2016 miniseries Roots and from 2018 to 2019 was regular cast member on the ABC legal drama For the People. In 2020, Page is starring in the Netflix period drama, Bridgerton.
