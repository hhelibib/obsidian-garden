皮德·蒙塔布姆·苏蒙瓦朗库尔Pide Monthapoom Sumonvarangkul是泰国歌手。他更广为人知的身份是二人组“皮得-马贡神风敢死队”的成员之一。他以前是RS集团下属的神风队成员，目前是Atime媒体下属的Atime 26成员，Atime媒体是GMM·格莱美的子公司。
Monthapoom Sumonvarangkul, nicknamed Pide, is a Thai singer. He is better known as one half of the duo "Pide - Magorn Kamikaze". He is formerly in Kamikaze under RS Group and currently a member of Atime 26 under Atime Media, a subsidary of GMM Grammy.
