## 人物介绍

### 生平
Dawson Towery is a stunt coordinator and performer recognized for his thoughtful approach to action design and his commitment to on-set safety and storytelling. Known for blending practical stunt experience with creative problem-solving, Towery works closely with directors, department heads, and performers to shape dynamic action that supports character and narrative, rather than just spectacle. With a background that spans physical performance, rigging environments, and logistical stunt planning, he is valued for his calm presence on set, clear communication, and ability to lead stunt teams through both preparation and execution. Whether working with large units or small specialty sequences, Towery focuses on building trust, efficiency, and professionalism across all levels of production. He continues to expand his work in film and television, developing grounded, intentional action sequences and fostering collaborative stunt environments.
