## 人物介绍

### 生平
A talented actor known for his infectious smile and laugh, Russell C. Gibbs was in the United States Marine Corps for 22 years. He rose to the rank of Master Sergeant. He served in multiple locations from New Orleans to Japan. After he retired from the Corps, he taught 7th-grade English and attended the University of Holy Cross in New Orleans to complete his Master's Degree.
