## 人物介绍

### 生平
MJ was born in Sydney, Australia and was raised on the Northern Beaches. Growing up he developed a love and passion for Film and TV and this led to him attending and graduating Screenwise Film & Tv School with a Diploma of Screen Acting in 2018. In 2019, MJ relocated to Vancouver, Canada to further pursue acting opportunities in North America before moving back to Australia in 2023. He is represented by The X Division and is best known for his work on 'Mr Inbetween', 'Chilling Adventures of Sabrina', 'Lewis Capaldi: Bruises', 'Two Sentence Horror Stories', 'The Right One', 'Year Of' and his upcoming portrayal of Sergei in the feature film 'Primitive War'. MJ also has credits on 'The Twilight Zone', 'Rules For Werewolves', 'The Stand', 'Bump' and 'Under The Bridge'.
