## 人物介绍

### 生平
Logan Pierce is a Canadian actor born Feb.16, 2011 in Vancouver, British Columbia.
At an early age he was recognized as a highly skilled athlete and musician, and landed his first acting role on his second ever audition for ABC's "The Good Doctor" in 2019.
Pierce appeared in HBO's premier episode of the global hit series "The Last Of Us". "When You're Lost In The Darkness" received critical acclaim and was watched by 4.7 million viewers on the first day and almost 40 million within two months.
Logan was recently cast in a large supporting role in the Hallmark TV movie "Mystery On Mistletoe Lane", which premiered on Nov. 09, 2023.
