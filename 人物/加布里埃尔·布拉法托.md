## 人物介绍

### 生平
Gabriel Burrafato is a dynamic, two-time Broadway World Award-winning actor whose screen presence and vocal power have captivated audiences on stage and screen. He is recognizable to TV audiences as "Alejandro," Chapa's hilarious dad, on the hit Paramount+ series Danger Force. Recent notable screen roles include "Reverend Edward Pike" in the season premiere of NCIS, "Mike Pomerantz" in Netflix's The Lincoln Lawyer alongside Neve Campbell, "Nick" on Young Sheldon, and "Joseph" opposite Zoe Saldaña in the acclaimed Netflix limited series From Scratch. Gabriel's career spans Broadway, Las Vegas, film, and television-earning him the reputation of a "Trilingual Triple Threat", fluent in English, Spanish, and Italian (with conversational French). He made his Broadway debut in Andrew Lloyd Webber's Bombay Dreams, and has starred in major national tours including Camelot as "Lancelot" and Evita (Harold Prince Revival), as "Magaldi". Gabriel originated the iconic role of "Legolas" in the $30 million world premiere stage production of The Lord of the Rings, directed by Tony Award winner Matthew Warchus. In Las Vegas, he also brought "Dean Martin" to life in Vegas! The Show at Planet Hollywood.
Gabriel's artistic training is as elite as his résumé: having studied voice at the Royal Conservatory of Music in Toronto, he began his professional journey as an apprentice actor at Canada's prestigious Shaw Festival. His mentors include legends such as Sir Andrew Lloyd Webber, two-time Academy Award-winner A.R. Rahman, and the late Harold Prince.
In addition to his dramatic and musical work, Gabriel is a seasoned comedic actor and improviser, having trained at UCB Theatre (Los Angeles) and performed with top improv troupes including UCB Mess Hall, Sin City Improv (Las Vegas), and Channel 101 (Los Angeles).
With an unparalleled range across genres, languages, and media, Gabriel Burrafato continues to be a force both in front of the camera and on the stage.
