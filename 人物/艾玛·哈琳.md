## 人物介绍

### 生平
Emma Halleen is an American/British actor.Her film debut was at Tribeca Film Festival, playing the role of August Blanco Rosenstien's little sister in Downtown Owl, a movie co-directed by Lily Rabe and Hamish Linklater (2023).She has worked as a lead actor with three AFI thesis films: Christmas, Dumplings and Tortellini (2022) Children of Change, The Erl King (2021), and Cal-Arts thesis film: Bind (2020).She studies under Charles Carpenter at Storytellers Conservatory.
