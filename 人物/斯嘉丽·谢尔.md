## 人物介绍

### 生平
Scarlett Sher is based in New York. She is a young actress who most recently guest stars as Harlyn in "Extrapolations" - playing the British daughter of Natasha (Gemma Chan) while looking up to Ezra (Tahar Rahim) as a father figure.
Scarlett made her film debut in "Hustlers" (2019) as Lily, the daughter of Destiny (Constance Wu). She plays Sam in the upcoming romantic comedy film "Asian Persuasion" - starring alongside KC Concepcion and Dante Basco and will also appear as Kiki in the dramatic film "Ramona at Midlife."
Scarlett also made her Broadway debut in Waitress the Musical as Lulu in 2019.
