### 生平
Patsy Ferran is a Spanish-British actress. She has received a number of accolades for her work in theatre, including a Laurence Olivier Award.
She read Drama and Theatre Arts at Birmingham University, and trained at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art, graduating in 2014.
