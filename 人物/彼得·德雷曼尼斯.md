## 人物介绍

### 生平
Peter Dreimanis co-founded Juno Award winning rock band July Talk. The band has toured internationally since 2013 and was the recipient of the 2017 Hi-Fidelity Prism Prize, awarded to a recording artist who has used video art to represent their work in a consistently creative and innovative way. He lives in Toronto.
