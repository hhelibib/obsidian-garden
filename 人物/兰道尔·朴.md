### 生平
Randall Park is a Korean American actor, writer, and director. He was born and raised in Los Angeles. Randall is a frequent contributor to Channel 101, and he performs live sketch comedy regularly with his friend Marques Ray. Randall can also be seen in television shows and movies. This is a collection of some of his independent stuff on the web. His favorite rapper is MF Doom and his favorite wrestler is George 'The Animal' Steele.
