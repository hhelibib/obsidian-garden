## 人物介绍

### 生平
Ed Weeks is a writer who first worked on the TV series Man Stroke Woman in 2005. Two years later, he made his acting debut the show Comedy: Shuffle. This was followed by roles in Phoo Action, Not Going Out, The IT Crowd, Hotel Trubble and Olivia Lee: Dirty, Sexy, Funny, where he also served as writer. In 2012, he became one of the main cast members of the comedy series The Mindy Project, which stars The Office actress Mindy Kaling.
