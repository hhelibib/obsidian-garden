## 人物介绍

### 生平
Robert Berlin American Method Actor. He works on Television, Film and in the Theatre. He is a recurring actor on TASK the HBO & HBO Max TV miniseries starring Mark Ruffalo created by Brad Inglesby. He played the crime lord Damian Glass acting with Dolph Lundgren the actor, director in the Castle Falls movie. Robert Berlin also played a dirty cop on 50 Cents show PowerBook III: Raising Kanan on Starz directed by Mario Van Peebles and played Jon Voight's cell mate in prison on Showtime's Ray Donovan. He can also be seen on NBC's The Blacklist and Law and Order SVU along with a lead role playing a drug running dirty cop on Lifetime's TV movie thriller Sisters on the Run produced by Andrew Stevens. He also gives seminars and 1 on 1 consultations online at his website. Robert Berlin is a Method Trained Actor who studied in New York City at Stella Adler Conservatory and with Actors Studio Method Acting teacher Tony Greco who taught David Harbour and Phillip Seymour Hoffman.
