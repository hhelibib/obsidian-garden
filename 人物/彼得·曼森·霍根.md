### 生平
Peter Hogan was born on June 25, 1998 in New York City, New York, USA. He is an actor, producer, artist manager and music supervisor known for Rushed (2021), The Eric Andre Show (2012) and Baby Mama (2008). Hogan is currently working on the upcoming film Shelter in Solitude (2021).
