## 人物介绍

### 生平
Aisha N. Tyler (born September 18, 1970)[1] is an American actress and comedian. She is known for playing Andrea Marino in the first season of Ghost Whisperer, Dr. Tara Lewis in Criminal Minds, Mother Nature in the Santa Clause films and voicing Lana Kane in Archer as well as recurring roles in CSI: Crime Scene Investigation, Talk Soup and Friends. She is formerly the co-host of CBS's The Talk and currently Whose Line Is It Anyway?.[2] She also hosted Ubisoft's E3 press conferences from 2012 to 2016 and has lent her voice and likeness to the video games Halo: Reach and Watch Dogs.
