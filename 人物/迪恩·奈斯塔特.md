## 人物介绍

### 生平
Dean Neistat is an American actor, stuntman and photographer. Before he began his career on screen he flew the C-17 Globemaster in the Active Duty United States Air Force for 7 years. He completed his service a decorated Instructor Pilot with more than 700 combat hours from missions flown in both Iraq and Afghanistan.
