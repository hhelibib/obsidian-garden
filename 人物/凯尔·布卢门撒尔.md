## 人物介绍

### 生平
Kyle Blumenthal is an American film, television, and theater actor born and raised in Philly. Kyle brings a grounded, emotionally intelligent presence to the screen. Known for playing characters who lead with heart and observation-his work is marked by quiet depth and nuanced vulnerability.
Whether playing a rookie ER doc, preppy boyfriend in trouble, or young underdog FBI agent, he carries a sharp emotional IQ and innate warmth that makes you root for him.
Equally at home on stage and film, he is also an award winning writer and director with a deep respect for all sides of the camera.
Something you may not know about Kyle is that he is a black belt in Tae Kwon Do and runs a chain of coffee shops in North Florida.
