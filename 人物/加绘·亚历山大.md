## 人物介绍

### 生平
Kae Yukawa, known professionally as Kae Alexander, is a British Japanese actress, best known for her role as Jing Hua in ‘Bad Education’ which she played from 2012–14 and in the 2015 film. She also played Leaf in the sixth season of ‘Game of Thrones’, and Linh Xuan Huy in ‘Collateral’.
