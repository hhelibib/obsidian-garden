## 人物介绍

### 生平
Born in 1995 to a Spanish father and Zimbabwean mother, Lou Llobell is a London-based actress known for her role as Gaal in Foundation (2021) and Zandie in Voyagers (2020). Raised between Spain and South Africa, Lou moved to the UK in 2013 to study Drama at the University of Birmingham, before later enrolling at Drama Centre London (Central St Martins, UAL)
