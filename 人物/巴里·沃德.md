## 人物介绍

### 生平
Barry Ward was born and brought up in Blanchardstown, a northwestern suburb of Dublin. He went to school at St. Declan's College in Cabra.
He is an actor noted for his performances on stage and screen, most recently as the leading man in the Ken Loach historical drama Jimmy's Hall.
