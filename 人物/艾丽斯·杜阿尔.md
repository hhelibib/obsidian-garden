## 人物介绍

### 生平
While studying directing at La Fémis, Alice Douard made Extrasystole, her graduation film, which was acquired by Arte and presented in many festivals. She went on to direct two other short films, Girls and Due West. In 2022, she completed another short film - Expecting - which won the Céar for Best Short Film. She later received the CNC screenwriting grant for Love Letters , her first feature film that expands on the themes of Expecting.
