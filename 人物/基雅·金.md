## 人物介绍

### 生平
Keeya King was born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. She is an actor known for Jigsaw (2017), Residue (2017), Private Eyes (2016), Anatomy of Assistance (2013), and Brown Girl In The Ring (2016). At a young age, she pursued a career in the arts studying at Lewis Baumander Acting Studio and Dean Armstrong Acting School.
