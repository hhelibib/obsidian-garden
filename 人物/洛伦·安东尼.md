## 人物介绍

### 生平
Bound by blood to the Chuska Mountains in the great southwest, Loren Anthony earned his position as a true modern day warrior for his people. Helping communities near and far, with his positive paying it forward mindset as the founder and organizer of Chizh For Cheii (Navajo for Firewood for Grandpa). Which has helped thousands of rural high risk elders on the Navajo Nation. This multi-talented artist/actor began his journey during his childhood, performing heavy metal music for the masses, touring nationally with his founding award winning band "Bloodline". Loren put music to the side to get sober, which spawned the non profit Chizh For Cheii. With new found life through sobriety, the entertainer within craved the art of performing. Acting became the new driving force which lead to motivational speaking events and promoting health education. Loren working closely with at risk youth and adults living with addictions, it was the love that fuels his fire. Returning to the stage by way for stand-up comedy and intimate acoustic sets, it was with the support of close friends, family and mentors that film put back into his veins. Loren's extrovert personality is undoubtedly contagious among those around him. Present day, with over 50 films under his belt, Loren continues to stand for what's right and follow his heart. The passion for acting is as strong as the passion for helping others. The road ahead is always unknown and Loren loves all challenges, he prevails with a smile on his face and laughter in his heart. Loren is currently the bassist for Navajo Nation's rising heavy metal band Hell Throne and that's just a tip of the iceberg that's on a long list of titles he holds. Loren's most greatest accomplishment is becoming a proud loving husband to his beautiful wife Shoshonia Anthony and proud father to their amazing daughter Kari. Love holds no bounds as life can only become more beautiful from here.
