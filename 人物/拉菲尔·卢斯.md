## 人物介绍

### 生平
Raphael Luce is a Franco-American actor known for playing Young Henry Creel / 001 in Season 4 of Stranger Things. He appears in Luc Besson’s upcoming feature Dracula, and his additional credits include Loki and The First Lady.
Born into a family of performers, Raphael is the son of stunt artists and former Cirque du Soleil members Jade Kindar-Martin and Karine Mauffrey. His siblings, Gabriel Sky and Jophielle Love, are also actors.
Raphael divides his time between Los Angeles, New Orleans, and France. Alongside acting, he trains extensively in stunt work, including high falls, screen fighting, full-body burns, drifting and stunt driving, and high-wire walking. He is fluent in French and maintains an active social media presence with more than 2 million followers.
