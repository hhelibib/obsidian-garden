## 人物介绍

### 生平
Matilda Firth is a British actress from Yorkshire. In 2022, Matilda filmed the role of Young Carole in Sky Studio's Christmas movie 'Christmas Carole'. This was followed by roles in Disney's Disenchanted and Vampire Academy.
In early 2023 she completed filming of a feature film, the second season of UK comedy sitcom Hullraisers and the second season of the acclaimed BBC show 'Time'.
