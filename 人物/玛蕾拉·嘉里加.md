### 生平
Mariela Garriga was born in Habana, Cuba. She has Italian, English, Spanish and France ancestry. She started her artistic career at the age of 13th. She professionally danced in theater and television at "Cuban Television Ballet" until she moved to Italy at a very young age where she stared to take part in some international advertising campaigns as a model.
