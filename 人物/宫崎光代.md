## 人物介绍

### 生平
HIKARI is an award-winning Japanese director, writer, and producer based in Los Angeles. Her feature debut, 37 Seconds (2019), had its world premiere at the 69th Berlinale, where it won the Panorama Audience Award and the CICAE Art Cinema Award, while also earning a nomination for Best First Feature. Most recently, she co-wrote and directed her second feature, Rental Family (2025), starring Brendan Fraser. The film premiered at the 50th Toronto International Film Festival, where HIKARI was honoured with the TIFF Emerging Talent Award. The film went on to gather audience awards around the USA, including at the Chicago International Film Festival. HIKARI also received the Directing Award from the Critics Choice Association for AAPI Cinema & Television, as well as the Filmmaker on the Rise Award at the Astra Film Awards. Her other work includes directing the pilot episode of the Emmy-winning Netflix series Beef (2023), episodes of Tokyo Vice (2023), and acclaimed short films such as Tsuyako (2011), which screened at over 100 international film festivals and received more than 50 awards, including the DGA Student Award. Rental Family is currently being released theatrically worldwide.
