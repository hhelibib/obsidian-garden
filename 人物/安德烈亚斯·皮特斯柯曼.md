## 人物介绍

### 生平
Andreas Pietschmann was born on March 22, 1969 in Würzburg, Bavaria, Germany. He is an actor, known for Echte Kerle (1996), Belle et Sébastien (2013) and GSG 9 - Die Elite Einheit (2007).
He lives with the actress Jasmin Tabatabai in Berlin and has with her a daughter who was born in July 2009, and a son, born on 13 August 2013.
