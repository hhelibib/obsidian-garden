## 人物介绍

### 生平
Londoner Charlotte was born on August 29th 1989 and attended James Allen's school in Dulwich. At age eleven she joined Youth Music Theatre UK, and toured Japan in their production of 'Pendragon' as well as appearing with the Young Pleasence Theatre at the Edinburgh Fringe in 2006. After a couple of small roles Charlotte was reading English and Drama at Bristol University when she successfully auditioned for the television sitcom 'Fresh Meat', and has since appeared in leading roles in another sitcom 'Siblings' and the period drama 'Call the Midwife'. In addition to acting Charlotte is a professional singer, a member of the female quartet All Angels, supporting Katherine Jenkins on her 2009 tour and performing at the UEFA Champions League final in 2011.
