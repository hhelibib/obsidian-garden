## 人物介绍

### 生平
Ewens Abid is a French actor of Algerian descent. Born in Belfort, Territoire de Belfort, France. He trained on the BA Acting (International) course at East 15 Acting School. Ewens Abid is an actor known for Crime (2021), Emily in Paris (2022), Tuesday (2023), The Last Rifleman (2023) and Take a Chance on Me (2023).
