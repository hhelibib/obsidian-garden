## 人物介绍

### 生平
Quyen Tran is an acclaimed cinematographer based in Los Angeles and New York, recognized for her emotionally resonant visual storytelling and her belief in always “prioritizing story above all else.”
Her feature film credits include Palm Springs (starring Andy Samberg and J.K. Simmons), which premiered at the 2020 Sundance Film Festival and set a record-breaking sale before becoming one of Hulu’s most streamed titles. She also shot The Little Hours, Dealin’ with Idiots, and several independent features praised for their warm, character-driven cinematography.
In television, Tran has worked on major projects such as the pilot of Unbelievable (Netflix), directed by Lisa Cholodenko and starring Kaitlyn Dever, Toni Collette, and Merritt Wever. Her other TV credits include Atypical, Maid, Parks and Recreation, and The Donor Party, showcasing her versatility across genres from comedy to drama.
Tran began her career as a photojournalist before transitioning into film, bringing a deep sense of humanity and authenticity to her visual style. She has collaborated extensively with documentary filmmaker Grace Lee, shooting the Peabody Award–winning American Revolutionary: The Evolution of Grace Lee Boggs. Her work has taken her to over a dozen countries, capturing stories across diverse cultures and landscapes.
Recognized as one of Variety’s “10 Cinematographers to Watch” and an ASC Rising Star, Quyen Tran continues to be a leading voice in contemporary cinematography—combining artistry, empathy, and technical mastery to bring powerful stories to life.
