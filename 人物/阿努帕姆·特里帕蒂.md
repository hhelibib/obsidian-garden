## 人物介绍

### 生平
Anupam Tripathi is an Indian actor who has appeared in various South Korean television series and films. Many of his roles relate to being a migrant worker in Korean society. His first feature film credit was on Ode to My Father (2014). He made his main cast debut as Ali Abdul on Netflix's survival drama series Squid Game (2021).
