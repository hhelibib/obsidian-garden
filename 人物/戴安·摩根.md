## 人物介绍

### 生平
Diane Morgan is an English actress, comedian, and writer. She has appeared on BBC comedy series Mock the Week and Charlie Brooker's Weekly Wipe and presented the mockumentaries Cunk on Christmas and Cunk on Shakespeare, as the comedy character Philomena Cunk. She also works with fellow comedian Joe Wilkinson as part of the deadpan sketch group "Two Episodes of Mash".
