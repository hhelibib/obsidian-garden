## 人物介绍

### 生平
纳修是一位来自泰国的流行民谣歌手。他的白马王子的长相、真诚的个性和甜美圆润的嗓音赢得了他的祖国许多女孩的心，他也被称为“泰国的李昇基”。他是《学院幻想曲》(AF5)第五季的赢家，也是一名活跃的音乐剧演员和模特。他的民谣在国内各种排行榜上名列前茅，他真诚而温柔的形象俘获了他祖国许多女性的心。
2012年，纳修开始了“新纳修”项目，飞往韩国继续他的职业生涯。该项目产生了单曲《她很坏》，以B2ST的龙俊亨为特色。
纳修此前曾参与另一部电视连续剧《九》的《外层空间》，该剧以韩文和泰文发行。
Natthew is a popular ballad singer from Thailand. His Prince Charming looks, sincere personality and sweet, mellow voice has captured the hearts of many girls in his home country, where he is also known as the ‘Lee Seung Gi of Thailand’. Winner of the fifth season of ‘Academy Fantasia’ (AF5), he is also an active musical theater actor and a model. His ballads have topped various domestic charts, and his sincere and gentle image has captured the hearts of many women in his home country.
In 2012, Natthew embarked on the ‘New Natthew’ project, flying over to Korea to further his career. The project resulted in the single, She’s Bad, featuring B2ST’s Yong Jun Hyung.
Natthew previously participated in the OST for another tvN drama, Nine, with the single, Oh Please, which was released in both Korean and Thai. (Source: mwave)
