## 人物介绍

### 生平
Michael Provost is known for his work on Insatiable (2018), #REALITYHIGH (2017) and Chance (2016).
Movie actor who appeared as Shannon in the 2017 Netflix film #REALITYHIGH. He also appeared in the 2017 TV series Chance and later Netflix's Insatiable in 2018.
Before Fame
His first on screen role was in the show Anonymous in 2011.
Trivia
He was the acting double for Asa Butterfield in Ender's Game.
Family Life
He has two older and two younger brothers.
Associated With
He appeared in #REALITYHIGH alongside Keith Powers.
