## 人物介绍

### 生平
The Argentine director and producer was born in 1978. He studied at the International Center of Photography in New York and subsequently film at the NYU Tisch School of the Arts. His second feature film Papirosen premiered in Locarno, won the Best Film Award at the film festival in Buenos Aires and was released in US cinemas. Kékszakállú won the FIPRESCI Prize for Best Film at Venice, where Introduzione all’oscuro was also presented. All his films have recently been acquired by MoMA.
