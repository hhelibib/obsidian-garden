### 生平
Stephen recently guest starred in Amazon's reboot of "Cruel Intentions" and shot a recurring role in "Gen V".
He starred in the independent feature films "Warrior Strong" and "Cascade". He also recurred on the CBC series "Essex County", which earned him a Canadian Screen Award nomination as part of the ensemble.
