## 人物介绍

### 生平
Kara Luiz was born in Bainbridge Island, Washington, USA. She is an actress and writer, known for Hacks (2021), The Night Watchmen (2017) and The Big Bang Theory (2007). She has been married to Oren Kaplan since May 6, 2015. They have one child.
