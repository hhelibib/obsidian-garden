## 人物介绍

### 生平
Rammy Park was born in Denver, Colorado and received her MFA in Film from Columbia University. She began her career as a journalist, with stints at ABC News, The Associated Press and NY1. A 2020 HBO Directing Fellow and a Film Independent Fellow (2018 Episodic Lab, 2019 Project Involve), she is also the recipient of grants from the National Board of Review, IFP, and the Caucus Foundation. She lives and works in Los Angeles.
