## 人物介绍

### 生平
Zeke Alton is an American actor and former US Navy Test Pilot. He was born in Brindisi, Italy and grew up on US military bases across Europe. He is the son of a US Air Force veteran and a British School teacher. He began performing in theater and indie film while still active duty in the military before moving to Los Angeles and making the transition to performing full-time. He studied acting at the Studio Theater Conservatory.
