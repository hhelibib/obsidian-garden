## 人物介绍

### 生平
A true bi-coastal talent, Brian D. Cohen transformed his roots as a child model and singer/songwriter/performer into a bustling acting career. Brian grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area, where he was exposed to all forms of art, film, media, performances and entertainment that only strengthened his desire to pursue his dreams. Little did he know that these dreams would eventually become a reality!
Brian made his television debut in the USA Network reboot of the classic Don Johnson series "Nash Bridges." From there his stock only rose with roles on such shows as "The Rookie" and "FBI." Among other things, Brian recently won "Best Supporting Actor" for his role in the acclaimed Lainie Kazan short "Greek Philosophia and Chickens."
Brian enjoys spending his time hiking, traveling, hanging with friends, reading scripts and spending time with his beautiful wife and vivacious little 2 year old daughter.
