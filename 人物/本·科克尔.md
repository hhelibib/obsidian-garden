## 人物介绍

### 生平
Ben Cockell is an actor, known for his recurring roles as Jackson in Disney Plus' The Mysterious Benedict Society (2021), and Young Morgan Edge in The CW's Superman and Lois (2021).
Ben is based in Vancouver and graduated from Vancouver Film School's Writing for Film and Television Program. He has written and directed short films (one of which won the Audience Favourite Award at Vancouver International Film Festival in the Youth Category and then toured across North America), and won the Top Development Grant at The Arts Club's LEAP Young Playwrights Program for his aquatic themed musical "Amputated Parts." He is currently working on a new short film titled "Family Photo" which he will star in.
