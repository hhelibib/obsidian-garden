### 生平
Jake Tapper was born on 12 March 1969 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA. He is an actor and writer, known for The Outpost (2019), Superman (2025) and Late Night (2019). He has been married to Jennifer Marie Brown since 3 September 2006. They have two children.
