## 人物介绍

### 生平
Nathan Castle is a South African born actor based in Vancouver, Canada. Known for his roles in Eraser: Reborn, Slumber Party Massacre and Reflection Point which he won best actor for at the LA International Festigious film festival.
He has landed roles on HBO's Raised By Wolves, Amazon Primes' the Power as well as the Netflix live action adaptation of One Piece.
He is a supporting lead in "The Nowhere Man" which will be released on Amazon Prime later this year(2025).
He has a passion for film making with experience in post production, stunts, production and now as a writer.
