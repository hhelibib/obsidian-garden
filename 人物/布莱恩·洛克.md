## 人物介绍

### 生平
Brian Rock was born and raised in the suburbs of Philadelphia. On any given day, you'd most likely find him running around the neighborhood pretending to be Batman.
Brian earned his SAG card with his first co-starring role in Dispatches From Elsewhere (created by and starring Jason Segel) followed by roles in Mare of Easttown, Law & Order: SVU, FBI, and Severance. Brian can be seen on NBC, HBO, APPLE TV, and CBS to name just a few.
Additionally, Brian has been featured in the independent short films including Ten Shots, The Signs, and 1805, which holds a special place in Brian's heart given his love of American history.
In his free time, Brian can be found trying to navigate life as a parent, listening to the same music he loved as a teenager, playing golf, and running around the neighborhood pretending to be Batman.
