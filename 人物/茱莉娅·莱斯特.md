### 生平
Los Angeles native Julia Lester has been professionally acting since the age of five. Partial credits include recurring roles on CBS's hit comedy "Mom", Netflix's "Prince of Peoria", and go90's "Mr. Student Body President". She is best known to audiences as Ashlyn in the Disney+ original series, "High School Musical: The Musical: The Series".
