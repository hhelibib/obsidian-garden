## 人物介绍

### 生平
Paul Piaskowski is an actor known for his depth, versatility, and fearless approach to complex roles. With over a decade of experience across U.S. and Canadian film and television, the Warsaw / Melbourne native-now based primarily in Vancouver-has built a career defined by range and unpredictability.
Beyond film and television, Paul is a seasoned theatre performer, having tackled powerhouse roles like Peter in Bug and Ariel in The Pillowman, making his Off-Broadway debut in 2019 as Daizy in Reborning at the SoHo Playhouse.
Whether on stage or screen, he thrives tackling complex, challenging roles that demand depth and nuance
