## 人物介绍

### 生平
He originally went to university to study law, but during his fourth year decided to move into acting. He won "Ellos i Elles", a Valencian T.V. show on Canal Nou, and used the 2 million pesetas (circa £10,000/ $15,000) prize money to pay for his studies at the American Academy of Dramatic Arts[4] He has developed his career in Spain and abroad.
Personal life
He has been married to the actress Cristina Peña and boyfriend to the Olympic swimmer Gemma Mengual.
