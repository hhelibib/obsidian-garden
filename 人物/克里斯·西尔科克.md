## 人物介绍

### 生平
Chris Silcox is an American Actor and Physical Performer. Born in San Antonio, Texas he trained as an Actor & Gymnast becoming an NCAA Division 1 All-American. After competing internationally for Team USA he helped create & perform in shows for Cirque Du Soleil including Viva Elvis and IRIS.
His theatrical and physical talents led him to a career in Hollywood where he has now appeared in over 80 films and television shows acting opposite Ewan McGregor, Ethan Hawke, Hugh Jackman, Zendaya, Anthony Mackie, Chloe Fineman, Stephanie Beatrice, Allison Tolman, Vondie Curtis Hall, Sophie Okonedo, Todd Louise, Maribel Verdu and Tom Holland.
As part of his film career he has portrayed the character of Leon in Raymond and Ray directed by Rodrigo Garcia, where Chris played one of the Brothers of the titular characters played by Ewan McGregor and Ethan Hawke. He also appeared as the Groom at the beginning of Wonder Woman 1984. Chris was Tom Holland's stunt double for Spider-Man Homecoming. His next Feature Film acting roles are in the films Joker: Folies A Duex and Incoming.
Chris Guest Starred as Scoey on an episode of KC Undercover opposite Zendaya and made a Guest appearance on Adam Ruins Everything as Peasant.
He has Co Stared on several TV Shows playing Eddie in Why Women Kill, Cyclist on Dave on FX, Bank Security Guard on Dark Winds and numerous other TV shows.
He has also appeared in other films and shows like: The Greatest Showman & The Wiz Live!
Chris has appeared in multiple ad campaigns and National Commericals for Apple, Nike, State Farm, Honda, Toyota, Liberty Mutual, Wells Fargo & Burger King!
