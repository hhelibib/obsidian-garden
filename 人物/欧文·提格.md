### 生平
Owen William Teague is an American actor. He was born and raised in Tampa, Florida, and is known for his roles in Bloodline and the episode "Arkangel" of season 4 of Black Mirror. Teague also appeared as Patrick Hockstetter in It and It Chapter Two.
