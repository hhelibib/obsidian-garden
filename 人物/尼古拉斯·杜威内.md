## 人物介绍

### 生平
Nicholas Duvernay is an American actor known for his versatility and on screen charisma. The actor, born in Long Island, New York, currently resides in Los Angeles, California.
Nicholas is often recognized by television viewers for his performance as Armando in Netflix's Purple Hearts (2022). After graduating high school and moving to Hollywood in 2019, Duvernay quickly secured his first film credit in HBO Max's My Dead Dad (2022). Nicholas, who once sported a head of long curls was prompted to shave his head in 2022, by Purple Hearts director Liz Allen, thus sparking a surge in interest around the industry. Duvernay would go on to acquire multiple guest star bookings including but not limited to; CW's Supernatural spin-off "The Winchesters" (2022-), Peacock's re-imagined "Fresh Prince" Drama, BEL-AIR (2022-) (recurring), BET's late night comedy "Assisted Living" (2020-) (multi-season arc) and ABC's crime drama "The Rookie" (2022).
Nicholas is represented by A3 Artists Agency, Rebel Creative Group and Gravity Hill Agency (Southeast)
