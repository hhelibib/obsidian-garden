## 人物介绍

### 生平
Huxley Fisher is both a dual Canadian and American actor, born in Calgary, Alberta Canada. Having his first role at 5 years old on the series, Heartland, he has since had roles on most notably to date: The Order starring Nicholas Hoult and Jude Law, Black Mirror Season 7, The Birds Who Fear Death, Night of the Reaper, and the upcoming series: Unspeakable: The Murder of Jon Benet Ramsey with Paramount Pictures.
