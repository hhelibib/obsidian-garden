## 人物介绍

### 生平
Nuala Peberdy is an upcoming British young actress and performer who has previously starred in the West End production of Mary Poppins (2019-2020) as Jane Banks.
Nuala has also been apart of prominent Film and Television such as Randall Miller's comedy 'Coffee Wars' (2023) as a young Kate Nash, Sky Original's TV series 'Unwanted' (2023) inspired by the book 'Bilal' and directed by Oscar Nominated Oliver Hirschbiegel, and most recently, featured as Orla in 'The Dumping Ground' (2025).
