## 人物介绍

### 生平
EMMY recipient, Wilbur T. Fitzgerald, is an actor, producer, and attorney, renowned for his dynamic performances in over 100 television shows and films. He lent his distinctive voice to MTV News and has narrated several acclaimed documentaries, showcasing his versatility and talent.
Recently, Wilbur captivated audiences in the Lionsgate feature *Are You There God? It's Me, Margaret*, sharing the screen with Oscar winner Kathy Bates, and in *The Neon Highway* alongside the legendary Beau Bridges.
Up next for Wilbur is the eagerly awaited reboot of *Naked Gun*, alongside Liam Neeson and Pamela Anderson. Wilbur's remarkable journey in the entertainment industry continues to inspire, proving that his passion and charisma are as boundless as ever.
