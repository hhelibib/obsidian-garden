## 人物介绍

### 生平
Rufus Jones was born in 1975 in London, England. He is an actor and writer, known for Paddington (2014), Holy Flying Circus (2011) and Mongrels (2010).
Trivia
Rufus is a member of the popular British sketch group 'The Dutch Elm Conservatoire'. They were nominated for a Perrier award at the 2005 Edinburgh fringe festival for their show 'Conspiracy'.
