## 人物介绍

### 生平
Robert Rhodes is an Actor from London, United Kingdom. He is best known for his role as Jimmy Jimmy in "28 Years Later" and the upcoming "28 Years Later: The Bone Temple. He played Silver Denys in "House of The Dragon" and appeared in 3 episodes of Apple TV's "Masters of The Air"
He appears as Dexter in "Reunion" available now on BBC IPlayer and in 2026 can be seen in Season 29 of the much loved BBC Crime Thriller "Silent Witness" and in cinemas, 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple and "Wasteman"
Robert is currently filming an exciting role for all 5 episodes of a New BBC Thriller to be released next year.
