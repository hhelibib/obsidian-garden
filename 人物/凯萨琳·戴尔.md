## 人物介绍

### 生平
In the last two decades Catherine Dyer has built an impressive resume on both sides of the camera. As an actress she is best known as the evil Agent Connie Frazier in season 1 of the Netflix mega-hit Stranger Things. Catherine also spent seven years as a Supervising Producer at A&E Networks garnering her a Primetime Emmy Award nomination in 2003.
Catherine is also the Director of three award winning shorts: Photo Bomb, The Audition and Lobster Trial.
Catherine is a graduate of The American Academy of Dramatic Arts in New York City and is a member of Actor's Equity, SAG-AFTRA and is a voting member of the Television Academy (Emmys). Catherine is a co-founder/owner of Atlanta acting studio, Drama Inc. and is on the faculty at the Terry Knickerbocker Studio in New York City.
