## 人物介绍

### 生平
Bodhi Rae Breathnach is a young English/ Irish actress quickly emerging as one of the most exciting new talents of her generation.
She makes her feature debut in Hamnet, directed by Academy Award-winner Chloé Zhao, playing Susanna, opposite Paul Mescal and Jessie Buckley.
She will next appear next year as Jessie, co-lead in Jason Statham's upcoming action-drama Shelter, directed by Ric Roman-Waugh.
Bodhi Rae will then be seen in the Autumn as Margaret Dashwood in Sense and Sensibility, directed by BAFTA-winner Georgia Oakley.
She also had a star making turn as Sarah in Beth Steel's, Olivier nominated, Till The Stars Come Down at the National Theatre.
Balancing natural emotional depth with a striking on-screen presence, Bodhi has already been praised by directors for her instinctive performances and professionalism beyond her years.
