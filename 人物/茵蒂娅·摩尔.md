## 人物介绍

### 生平
Born in the Bronx, she has appeared in Vogue and OUT, as well as assignments for GQ, Gucci and Dior. In 2017, Indya performed alongside Katy Perry and Migos for Saturday Night Live. She acted in the feature film Saturday Church which premiered at the Tribeca Film Festival and is distributed by Samuel Goldywn Films. She has been featured in international music videos including J. Views "Don't Pull Away" and Moses Sumner's "Quarrell." Indya plays the role of "Angel" in Ryan Murphy's FX series Pose which debuts in Summer 2018.
