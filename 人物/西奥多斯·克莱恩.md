## 人物介绍

### 生平
Originally from Atlanta, Ga, Theodus got his start in musical theater before transitioning to film and television. With roles ranging from post-apocalyptic survivor, Big Tiny on AMC's The Walking Dead to the brooding warrior Zeke in the acclaimed series Underground, and the affable Jeremiah in Five Nights At Freddy’s 1 & 2, as well as his first feature-length starting turn as Jenks in Don’t Die. Theodus has undeniable screen presence and breathes life into every character he plays. With a martial arts background dating back to his early teens, Theodus developed a love for stunts on his first stunt job doubling Nonso Anosi for 3 seasons on the sci-fi series Zoo on CBS and has since gone on to lend his talents to projects like Survivors Remorse, Lucifer and, most recently, Theodus did stunts as well as the critically acclaimed movie Sinners. enjoys the unique challenge of Stunt/Actor roles. Theodus is also a UCBNY alum with stand-up and voice-over experience.
