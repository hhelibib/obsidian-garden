## 人物介绍

### 生平
Sarah Anne Williams is an American voice actress who provides voices for video games, anime, audiobooks and commercials. She was born in Indiana, and currently resides in Los Angeles.In anime, she is known for her performances as Sayaka Miki in Puella Magi Madoka Magica, Nonon Jakuzure in Kill la Kill, Lisbeth in Sword Art Online, Neferpitou in Hunter x Hunter, Karin Miyoshi in Yuki Yuna is a Hero, Puck in the 2016 version of Berserk, and Yuniko Kozuki in Accel World. In video games, she is known for her performances as Jinx in League of Legends, Uni / Black Sister in the Hyperdimension Neptunia series, Echo in Mobius Final Fantasy, Jack Frost in Shin Megami Tensei IV: Apocalypse, Chihaya Mifune in Persona 5, Peacock in Skullgirls, and Mist in Fire Emblem Heroes.
