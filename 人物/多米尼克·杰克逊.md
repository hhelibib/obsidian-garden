## 人物介绍

### 生平
Dominique Brebner (born March 20, 1965) known professionally as Dominique Jackson and Tyra Allure Ross is a Trinidadian-American transgender actress, author, model, and reality television personality. As an actress, she is most known for her leading role of Elektra Abundance on the FX television series, Pose. As a model, she has appeared in Vogue España.
