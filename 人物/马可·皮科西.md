## 人物介绍

### 生平
One of the most important artists of his generation, Marco Pigossi collects high-profile roles in 13 years of career as an actor. Is one of the main characters in "A Força do Querer", the most successful prime time soap opera of Rede Globo in the last 5 years. He is doing his debut in the film industry this year, as the main character in two movies: "A Última Chance" direct by Paulo Thiago and "O Nome da Morte", direct by Henrique Goldman. Marco has worked on big soap operas on TV, such as "A Regra do Jogo" (2015), "Boogie Oogie" (2014), "Sangue Bom" (2013), "Gabriela" (2012), "Fina Estampa" (2011), "Ti Ti Ti" (2010), "Caras & Bocas" (2009), "Queridos Amigos" (2008), "Eterna Magia" (2007) and "Um Só Coração" (2004).
