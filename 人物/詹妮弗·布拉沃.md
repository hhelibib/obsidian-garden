### 生平
Jennifer Bravo, born Jennifer Bravo Hernandez, is a Mexican-American Actress. She was born and raised in the South suburbs of Illinois and Studied Communication and Acting at Columbia College Chicago. Jennifer now currently lives in Los Angeles, California. At a young age she fell in love with performing arts and has continued her journey ever since.
