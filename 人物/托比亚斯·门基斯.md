## 人物介绍

### 生平
Hanan Tobias Simpson Menzies is an English stage, television and film actor. He is best known for his dual roles of Frank and Jonathan "Black Jack" Randall in STARZ's Outlander, which earned him a Golden Globe Award nomination, in addition to his roles as Brutus in HBO's Rome and Edmure Tully in HBO's Game of Thrones. Menzies portrays Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh in the third and upcoming fourth seasons of Netflix's series The Crown.
