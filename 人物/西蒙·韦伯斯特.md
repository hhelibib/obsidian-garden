## 人物介绍

### 生平
Simon Webster is a rising young actor best known for his series regular role as Ethan Matthews in the MGM+ hit series FROM.He began his career with a recurring role as Fred, a young orphan, in nine episodes of Hallmark Channel's WHEN HOPE CALLS, the spin-off of WHEN CALLS THE HEART. Since then, Simon has built an impressive resume with appearances in high-profile television series, including Quibi's MOST DANGEROUS GAME (starring Liam Hemsworth), National Geographic's THE HOT ZONE, SyFy's CHUCKY, and NBC's BRILLIANT MINDS, where he played the younger version of Zachary Quinto in a recurring guest star role. He will next be seen in THE Z SUITE, premiering later this year and THE LOVE CLUB, in a recurring role.Beyond his on-camera work, Simon is a seasoned voice actor, bringing to life Pickles in Disney Junior's SUPERKITTIES, Alex in Spin Master's PAW PATROL, and the title role in 80 episodes of Cartoon Network's LUCAS THE SPIDER.On the big screen, Simon stars in the feature film THE INVISIBLES, alongside Gretchen Mol, Tim Blake Nelson, and Bruce Greenwood, currently available on Prime Video. Up next, he will appear in a supporting role opposite Dakota Johnson and Adria Arjona in SPLITSVILLE.At just 12 years old, Simon is based in Toronto, Canada. When he's not acting, he enjoys a variety of sports, with a special love for soccer, tennis, skating, baseball, water-skiing, and swimming.
