## 人物介绍

### 生平
加里·维纳查克是美国著名连续创业企业家，四次荣登《纽约时报》畅销书作者，，演说家和国际公众人物。早年他通过在品酒行业把家族企业从3百万的生意翻倍至6千万美金从而被人所熟知，现在他被大家视为数字营销与社交媒体的先驱，在纽约创立维纳媒体和维纳X。
同时，维纳查克也是一位天使投资人和顾问，参与领投过Uber、Birchbox、Snachat、Facebook、Twitter 及 Tumblr等。他现也经常作为主要发言人活跃在各大国际科技和企业家的高峰会中。
Gary Vaynerchuk is an American serial entrepreneur, four-time New York Times bestselling author, speaker and internationally recognized internet personality. First known as a leading wine critic who grew his family’s wine business from $3 million to $60 million, Vaynerchuk is best known as a digital marketing and social-media pioneer at the helm of New York-based VaynerMedia and VaynerX.
Vaynerchuk is an angel investor or advisor to Uber, Birchbox, Snapchat, Facebook, Twitter and Tumblr, among others. He is a regular keynote speaker at global entrepreneurship and technology conferences.
