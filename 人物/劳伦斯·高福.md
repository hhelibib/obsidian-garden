## 人物介绍

### 生平
Lawrence Gough is an esteemed British film and TV director and writer, renowned for his compelling storytelling and dynamic direction. Born in England in 1970, Lawrence spent his formative years traveling across America with his family, an experience that enriched his creative perspective. He burst onto the filmmaking scene by clinching the Best Newcomer Award at the Edinburgh Film Festival in 2009 with his debut, Salvage. Lawrence's significant strides in the industry include directing a variety of acclaimed Prime Time TV shows, such as Misfits, Endeavour, Doctor Who, and Vera. His filmography also features Gatecrash, a dark, psychological thriller. His distinguished career continues to evolve with his latest projects, including being the lead director of the international thriller Band of Spies and earning a BAFTA nomination for The Gold, marking him as a prominent figure in contemporary cinema and television.
