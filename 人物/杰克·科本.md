## 人物介绍

### 生平
Jake Coburn is currently a Co-Showrunner and Executive Producer of NBC's The Hunting Party (2025). Before that, he was Co-Creator and Executive Producer of NBC's The Endgame (2022), Executive Producer on Netflix's Barbarians (2022), Netflix's Biohackers (2021), and ABC's Quantico. He began his television writing career on ABC's critically-acclaimed drama Dirty Sexy Money and then segued to Gossip Girl where he rose to the level of Supervising Producer. He has also written for the CW's Arrow and Dynasty. In addition to writing TV, Coburn has published two award-winning teen novels. A graduate of Brown University, he lives in Los Angeles.
