## 人物介绍

### 生平
Necar Zadegan is an American actress. She became known for her recurring role as First Lady Dalia Hassan on the eighth season of 24 and Delia Banai on Girlfriends' Guide to Divorce. Zadegan made her Broadway debut alongside Robin Williams in the Pulitzer Prize–nominated production Bengal Tiger at the Baghdad Zoo in 2011. She is fluent in English, German and Persian languages.
