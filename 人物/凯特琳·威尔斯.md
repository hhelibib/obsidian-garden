## 人物介绍

### 生平
Katelyn Logan Wells is a Canadian-born actress and award-winning screenwriter. At 14 years old she signed with her first agent and began her on-screen career while attending Ottawa's prestigious Canterbury Arts High School. Katelyn is best known for playing LGBTQ fan-favorite Silver in the highly acclaimed Netflix series Ginny & Georgia (2021) and Samara Alderton in Netflix's original series The Abandons (2025).Katelyn has collaborated with some of the most respected actors and filmmakers in the industry. From working alongside Brie Larson on the academy award-winning film Room (2015), to starring opposite Lena Headey and Gillian Anderson in an 1850’s Western, Katelyn has a proven track record of holding her own next to some of Hollywood's heaviest hitters.
