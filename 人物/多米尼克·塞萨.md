## 人物介绍

### 生平
Dominic Sessa is an American actor best known for his performance as troubled teenager Angus Tully in Alexander Payne's coming-of-age film The Holdovers (2023).
Sessa attended high school at Deerfield Academy in Deerfield, Massachusetts.Sessa is currently a student at the Carnegie Mellon School of Drama.
