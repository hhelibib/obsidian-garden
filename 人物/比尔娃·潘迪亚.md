## 人物介绍

### 生平
Birva is a Canadian actress known for Magic School Bus, Northern Rescue, Ransom, Night Raiders, Transplant, Minority Report (Pilot), Odd Squad and many more good shows. She has also appeared in few commercials such as Cisco (Competitors), Toronto Public Library (PSA) and Maple Leaf Food (Surgeon). She is passionate for acting, singing dancing, and modeling. Birva has a great passion for Bollywood dancing and took special training from Shaimak Davar International Dance School. She has also taken training from various Acting schools in Toronto including workshops that focuses on special skills such as acting, voice-over, dancing, modeling, martial-art etc. She is Happy-GO-Girl and in her free time she loves reading, swimming, skating, dancing and baking.
