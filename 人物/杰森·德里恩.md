## 人物介绍

### 生平
Jason Deline is a Canadian actor, voice artist, voice coach, comedian, and director with a career spanning animation, film, television, and commercial work. His journey began in high school with musical theater, where he was the youngest cast member in his production of Little Shop of Horrors. This early experience led to a role in the Canadian teen series Ready or Not (1997). He later pursued radio broadcasting, gaining experience in voice-over and commercial work.
