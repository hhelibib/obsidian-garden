## 人物介绍

### 生平
Julien Gaspar-Oliveri trained at the National Conservatory of Dramatic Arts as he started working in cinema. His mid-length short, Villeperdue, was critically acclaimed and was released in cinemas. Tender Age was nominated for a César for Best Fiction Short Film. In 2024, his Arte mini-series Those Who Blush won the Award for Best Series at Série Mania, and was selected for Cannes Séries. Stonewall is his first feature film.
