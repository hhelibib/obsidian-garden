## 人物介绍

### 生平
导演、编剧，西安人。毕业于美国哥伦比亚大学电影导演系研究生，美国俄亥俄大学影视制作系本科。2018年拍摄芒果娱乐《大明锦衣》、2019年拍摄华策影视《大铸剑师》。2019年入选京都电影人训练营，为东映株式会社拍摄日本古装时代剧《猫与猪与酒》。此外，李雨夕也曾在纽约李·斯特拉斯伯格、对邻里剧院学校进修戏剧表演，并在古琴、剑道、咏春方面颇有研究。
Chinese director and writer Yuxi Li graduated from Columbia University with an MFA in Film Directing in 2018 and from Ohio University with BSc in TV and Film Production in 2014. Her filmography includes “Blades of the Dark Knight” (Mango Entertainment, 2018), “Sword of the Destiny” (Huace Media, 2019), in addition to a track history of award-winning short films. In 2019, she attended the Kyoto Filmmaker Lab and directed Japanese period drama / jidaigeki “A Cat, a Pig, and Sake” (Toei Co., 2019). Additionally, Yuxi Li attended acting training at the Lee Strasberg Theatre & Film Institute and the Neighborhood Playhouse School of the Theater in New York, with a strong interest in Chinese zither, kendo swordplay, and Wing Chun.
