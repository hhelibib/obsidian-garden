Mini Bio (1)
Madelyn Cline was born in 1997, in Charleston, South Carolina, where she has grown up and still resides. Madelyn first stepped on stage as a way to overcome her stage fright but quickly fell in love with acting. Shortly thereafter she met with New York agent, Doris Stinga, with FFT Inc. and for several years after, she split her time between Charleston and New York. It was during her summers in New York she worked for and appeared in multiple TV commercials and many print ads for clients such as Next, T-mobile, Sunny D, and many more.
Most recently, Madelyn has worked primarily in the southeastern film market in leading and supporting roles such as Willow in Savannah Sunrise, Robin in The Reason and as Taylor Watts in HBO's Vice Principals.
