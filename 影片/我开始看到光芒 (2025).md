## 简介
The film will follow Ezra, a grief-stricken jazz trumpeter who seeks purpose and salvation after the sudden, tragic death of his wife and daughter. When he is mistaken for the new lighthouse keeper, Ezra meets Hannah, a local school teacher who, along with ethereal music, reignites his passion for life and imbues it with meaning.
